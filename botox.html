About the procedure

Botulinum toxin injections are a non-surgical treatment that temporarily reduces targeted muscle activity to soften dynamic wrinkles and fine lines while maintaining natural facial expression.

What it can address Treatment can soften expression-related lines such as forehead lines, frown lines between the eyebrows, and crow's feet around the eyes.
How it works The treatment temporarily reduces nerve signals to selected muscles, allowing them to relax and making associated wrinkles less visible.
Timing and duration Results develop gradually over several days and commonly last around three to six months, depending on the individual and treatment area.
